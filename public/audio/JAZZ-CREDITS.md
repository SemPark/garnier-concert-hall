## Peak normalization (2026-11-11)

The eight active Christmas tracks use constant gain, preserving their existing dynamics. Re-encoded MP3 true peaks were measured at −1.7 to −1.2 dBTP, below the −1 dBTP ceiling. Original pre-adjustment files and measurements are retained locally in archive/audio-before-peak-normalization/.
