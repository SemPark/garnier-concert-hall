Modifications: Earth's surface and cloud maps are blended into one 1024-pixel-wide texture, with thinner clouds near the Korean peninsula. The Jupiter and Saturn maps were resized to 1024 pixels wide and the other planet and Moon maps to 512 pixels wide. The maps wrap onto spheres, with baked vertex shading on the planets and Moon. Ring alpha is thresholded. No AI modification.
